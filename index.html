About

I am a hydrologist working on data-driven methods for environmental and water systems, combining hydrological and hydrodynamic modelling, data assimilation, GIS, and remote sensing. My work uses machine learning and mathematical frameworks to improve forecasting and our understanding of how these systems behave.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher and Data Assimilation Lead at the Data-driven Computing in Civil Engineering (DCC) group, RWTH Aachen University, on the FLAIR project — developing an AI-based approach to predict ensembles of regional flood flows and inundation depth. Before Aachen I was an NCEO fellow at the University of Reading, where I carried out the first state–parameter assimilation for the JULES land surface model using hybrid En-Var assimilation of COSMOS-UK soil moisture observations, and assimilated streamflow into CaMa-Flood to improve UK flood inundation forecasts.

I hold a PhD in Remote Sensing from IIT Bombay, where my thesis developed efficient assimilation strategies for hydrological models.

My full curriculum vitae, with the complete list of publications, awards, and service, is available as a PDF download.
